Product roadmap

Progress from product foundations to AI-assisted operations.

The product vision supports staged investment that expands capability while preserving governance and human decision authority.

Stage 1 · Product foundation

Establish and expand the core platform

Build the reusable foundation across provider management, licensing, inspections, background checks, portals, child care search, QRIS, reporting, document management, subsidy, eligibility, attendance, payments, workforce, credentialing, Pre-K, referrals, family services, and broader operational analytics.

Stage 2 · AI assistance

Introduce governed AI capabilities

Support intake, document review, summarization, inspection preparation, complaint triage, exception review, help desk work, and data-quality flagging with human oversight, auditability, privacy controls, and decision governance.
